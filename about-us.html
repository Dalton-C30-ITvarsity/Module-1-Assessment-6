About Us

At Cookie Crumble, we bring the joy of freshly baked, artisanal cookies straight from our cozy kitchen to your doorstep.

As a small, passionate startup, we specialize in crafting uniquely delicious cookies that are perfect for satisfying a sweet craving, elevating your coffee break, or becoming the star of your care package.

Our menu features an ever-rotating selection of gourmet flavors, from classic Chocolate Chunk with a sea salt finish to innovative creations like Brown Butter Toffee and Lemon Blueberry Cheesecake.

Every cookie is made in small batches using high-quality, locally-sourced ingredients whenever possible, ensuring that each bite is packed with flavor and baked with love.

We’re more than just a treat; we’re a small business dedicated to creating moments of happiness, one perfect, crumbly cookie at a time.
